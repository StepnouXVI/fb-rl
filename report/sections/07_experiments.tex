\section{Experimental Evaluation and Analysis}

\subsection{Experimental Setup and Benchmark Protocol}
All experiments were conducted on the official OGBench benchmark \cite{ogbench2024} environment \texttt{ogbench-antmaze-medium-navigate-v0}. To ensure statistical significance and rule out seed cherry-picking, we evaluated all candidate planners across \textbf{10 independent random seeds} (Seeds 1--10) with 10 evaluation episodes per task across all 5 official evaluation tasks (total of \textbf{500 episodes per method}):
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,noitemsep]
    \item \textbf{Task 01 \& 02}: Straight long-distance corridors with single $90^\circ$ turns;
    \item \textbf{Task 03 \& 04}: Multi-junction corridors with narrow bottlenecks and hairpin turns;
    \item \textbf{Task 05}: Global diagonal transit across the entire maze layout.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Key Quantitative Findings and Analysis}

\subsubsection{1. Superiority of Enhanced Sequence Reasoning (88.0\%)}
\texttt{EnhancedSequenceWaypointAttentionPlanner} achieves the highest overall success rate (\textbf{88.0 $\pm$ 5.2\%}), outperforming both the single-intention baseline (+6.2\%) and the raw Dijkstra teacher with \texttt{high\_actor} (+4.4\%). Crucially, on narrow corridors and hairpin turns (Task 01: 89.0\%, Task 03: 86.0\%, Task 04: 77.0\%), the combination of ALiBi distance decay and curvature tokens effectively eliminates wall-collision failures.

\subsubsection{2. Ultra-Fast $O(1)$ Amortized Distillation (85.0\%, 0.56 ms)}
The feedforward \texttt{DistilledJAXPlanner} achieves \textbf{85.0 $\pm$ 3.5\%} success rate with the \textbf{lowest variance across seeds} ($\sigma = 3.5\%$) and a step latency of only \textbf{0.56 ms}. By learning the direct mapping $(s_t, B(g)) \to z_{\text{cmd}}$ end-to-end, it achieves State-of-the-Art performance with zero graph storage or graph traversal at inference time.
