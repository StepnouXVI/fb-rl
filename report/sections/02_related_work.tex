\section{Related Work}

\subsection{Successor Representations and Forward-Backward Factorization}
The successor representation (SR) pioneered by Dayan \cite{dayan1993improving} decouples environment dynamics from reward functions. Touati et al. \cite{touati2021learning} extended this to continuous state-action spaces by framing successor measures as the solution to a matrix factorization problem in Hilbert spaces. The FB representation models:
\begin{equation}
    M(s_0, s') = \sum_{t=0}^\infty \gamma^t P^\pi(s_t = s' \mid s_0) \approx F(s_0, z)^\top B(s')
\end{equation}
While FB representations excel at reward generalization, their local Euclidean structure fails when evaluating connectivity across disconnected manifold regions separated by impassable obstacles.

\subsection{Hierarchical Goal-Conditioned Reinforcement Learning}
Hierarchical RL methods decompose long-horizon tasks into two levels: a high-level manager that emits intermediate subgoals $w_t \in \mathcal{S}$ or intentions $z_t \in \mathbb{R}^d$, and a low-level worker $\pi^\ell(a \mid s, w)$ that tracks subgoals over short horizons $\tau$ (e.g., HIQL \cite{park2023hiql}). 

In standard FB-based hierarchical policies (e.g., Switching FB), the high-level policy $\pi^h(z_w \mid s, z_g)$ is trained via advantage-weighted regression:
\begin{equation}
    \mathcal{L}_{\text{Plan}}(\pi^h) = -\mathbb{E}\left[\exp\left(\alpha \cdot \text{Adv}(s, w, g)\right) \log \pi^h(z_w \mid s, z_g)\right]
\end{equation}
However, when $g$ is far beyond the effective horizon of the successor measure, $\pi^h$ collapses into a single-step greedy heuristic that is unable to compose multi-hop paths.

\subsection{Topological Graph Navigation}
Graph-based planning over offline replay buffers constructs a discrete roadmap where nodes are dataset states and edges denote local reachability. By querying graph search algorithms (e.g., Dijkstra, $A^*$), global paths can be established across arbitrarily complex mazes. However, standard graph methods incur $O(N^2)$ distance evaluations and suffer from high test-time latency and dependency on raw waypoint tracking fidelity.
