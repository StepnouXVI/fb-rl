\section{Conclusion and Future Directions}

In this research, we tackled the fundamental challenge of horizon collapse and barrier attraction in zero-shot Forward-Backward (FB) representations on complex navigation mazes. By decoupling topological reasoning from low-level torque execution, we introduced:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,noitemsep]
    \item An offline topological reachability graph (\texttt{BufferGraphPlanner}) constructed over frozen FB embeddings that finds global multi-hop shortest paths without generative world models;
    \item An end-to-end differentiable $O(1)$ amortized planner (\texttt{DistilledJAXPlanner}) that achieves \textbf{85.0\%} success rate with \textbf{0.56 ms} inference latency via physics-grounded backpropagation through frozen $\pi^\ell$ and $F$;
    \item An Enhanced Sequence-Aware Attention Transformer (\texttt{EnhancedSequencePlanner}) that incorporates ALiBi distance-decay attention bias, local-global windowing, and curvature encodings, setting a new State-of-the-Art benchmark of \textbf{88.0 $\pm$ 5.2\%} across 10 random seeds.
\end{enumerate}

All code, checkpoints, configurations, and evaluation suites are publicly available at \texttt{github.com/StepnouXVI/fb-rl} under branch \texttt{optimization-and-translator}.
