\begin{abstract}
Zero-shot goal-conditioned reinforcement learning using Forward-Backward (FB) representations allows executing diverse tasks without task-specific retraining. However, in long-horizon navigation benchmarks such as \texttt{antmaze-medium}, standard hierarchical controllers $\pi^h(s_t, B(g))$ suffer from severe \textit{horizon collapse}: a single latent intention $z$ cannot negotiate multi-hop topological corridors separated by physical walls, causing agents to get trapped in local potential wells (78.8\% success rate). In this paper, we systematically investigate, formulate, and benchmark three complementary paradigms that solve this challenge using frozen FB representations without world models or online environment interactions:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,noitemsep]
    \item \textbf{Topological Graph Planning (\texttt{BufferGraphPlanner})}: Constructs an offline reachability graph $M(s, g) = F(s, B(g))^\top B(g)$ over dataset landmarks and extracts optimal multi-hop paths via Dijkstra's algorithm.
    \item \textbf{Amortized $O(1)$ Latent Plan Distillation (\texttt{DistilledJAXPlanner})}: Compresses global Dijkstra paths into an end-to-end differentiable Gated Cross-Attention student network trained via JAX/XLA backpropagation through frozen policy $\pi^\ell$ and forward representations $F$, reaching \textbf{85.0 $\pm$ 3.5\%} success rate with \textbf{0.56 ms} inference latency.
    \item \textbf{Enhanced Sequence-Aware Attention Transformer (\texttt{EnhancedSequencePlanner})}: Incorporates ALiBi distance-decay attention bias ($-\lambda m_h k$), local-global windowing, curvature turn encodings, and auxiliary local anchoring to prevent wall-leakage, achieving State-of-the-Art performance of \textbf{88.0 $\pm$ 5.2\%} across 10 random seeds.
\end{enumerate}
We provide full mathematical derivations, physical gait analyses of MuJoCo torque control, ablation studies, and comprehensive Q\&A preparation for technical defense.
\end{abstract}
